How it works

Three simple steps

The platform's job is discovery — once a need is claimed, the actual help happens directly between the NGO and the institution.

1. Post it

An approved institution posts a need — a category, a short description, nothing more.

2. Find it

Approved NGOs browse the board by category and location, and see it right away.

3. Claim it

An NGO claims the need. Its status updates on the board so effort isn't duplicated.

Why manual review

Every account is checked by hand

Both institutions and NGOs are reviewed before their listing or account goes live. This keeps the board trustworthy for the people relying on it — no fake institutions, no fake NGOs, and no individual resident details are ever collected or posted.
